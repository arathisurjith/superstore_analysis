Superstore Sales Analysis

📌 Project Overview

The Superstore Sales Analysis project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to understand business performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product profitability, and regional sales trends.

The main objective is to extract meaningful insights from the Superstore dataset using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) is performed to identify sales patterns, profit trends, and business opportunities.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze overall sales and profit.
Identify the best-selling product categories and sub-categories.
Understand regional sales performance.
Analyze customer purchasing behaviour.
Identify the most profitable products.
Examine monthly and yearly sales trends.
Understand the relationship between sales, quantity, discount, and profit.
Generate meaningful business insights through data visualization.

📂 Dataset Description

The Superstore dataset contains retail transaction information.
